Story board

About a young boy playing video games until a vile virus like creature ends his playtime short and will do anything to kill him in the real world.

Endings:
1. Lazy Ending: You did not turn on the lights and returned to your game and Entity-0565 does unspeakable things to you (you die a brutal death)
2. Aggressive Ending: You kill the enemy by burning it alive, you live but you now have severe PTSD
3. Normal Ending: You completed the intended story of the game, however Entity-0565 jumpscares you and you die
4. Escape Ending: You successfully evade the creature and its attacks, it does not kill you, you do not die, you just escape
5. Suicide Ending: In attempt of killing the monster by burning it alive in your house, you do not get out within the time interval, both you and the monster meet an firey demise
6. Peaceful Ending: The monster never attacks you or pursues you, you live to see another day and thats it (fucking loser)
